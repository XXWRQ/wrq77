The most common problem with a personal knowledge system is not that the tool is too simple. It is that capture and use are too far apart.

## Work backwards from use

Ask where the material is likely to reappear: in writing, a project decision, a review, or a presentation. Once the destination is clear, capture gains a useful standard.

## Keep three actions

1. Capture: save a signal worth revisiting.
2. Clarify: explain in your own words why it matters.
3. Connect: place the new idea inside an existing question or theme.

## Make the system smaller

Old interests, duplicate references, and unused categories can go. The value of the system comes from being able to find, understand, and reuse an idea—not from the number of entries it contains.
